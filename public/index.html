STUN servers

        
        
One per line. STUN only tells Miserecord your public address so you and your friend can find each other.
          Your audio, video and files never pass through it. Clear the box to stay on your local network with no outside servers.


        
Relays for calling saved friends

        
        
One per line. Relays only pass encrypted "I'm calling" messages and connection info between you and a saved friend.
          They can't read them and don't store them, but they can see your IP address.
